Snip AITerms of Use

Privacy Policy

Effective November 19, 2026

Snip AI ("Snip AI", "we", "us") is an iPhone app that records or imports your talking videos and cuts out the pauses, filler words and retakes. It is operated by JOH LLC. This policy explains what information the app collects, why, who we share it with, how long we keep it, and the choices you have.

The short version: to cut a video, the app sends its audio to a transcription service and the transcript to an AI model. If you use cloud or team features, your videos are stored in our cloud. We do not sell your information, we do not show ads, and we do not track you across other companies' apps or websites.

1. Information we collect

Account information

You sign in with Apple or Google. We receive your email address and, for Google accounts, your name (and the Google SDK may fetch your profile photo to show in the app). If you use Sign in with Apple's "Hide My Email", we receive only the relay address Apple gives us. We assign your account an internal user ID.

Your videos, audio and transcripts

  • Audio. When you finish a recording or import a video to cut, the app uploads its audio track so it can be transcribed. Transcription is done by our providers (see section 3). If the cloud services are unavailable, newer iPhones can transcribe on the device instead.
  • Transcripts and cut decisions. The transcript, with word timings, is sent to an AI model that decides what to cut. We store the transcript and the resulting cut with the job so it can be retried and reviewed.
  • Videos. Your videos are stored on your iPhone. If you use cloud storage, sharing or team features (for example, handing a project to an editor), your takes, the project file, its cover image and the finished video are uploaded to our cloud storage so that you and the people on your team can open them. Projects on your iPhone are excluded from iCloud and computer backups.
  • Photos, videos and voice-overs you add to a project, such as overlays, are handled like the rest of the project: on your iPhone, and in our cloud only when the project is stored in the cloud.

"Improve cuts with my fixes" (off unless you turn it on)

If you turn this setting on, when you restore something the AI removed or otherwise correct a cut, we receive that correction together with that take's full transcript and how it was cut, never its video or audio. We use these examples to test and improve how Snip AI cuts, which may include sending them to our AI provider to evaluate new versions. You can turn the setting off at any time in Settings; it stops future sharing.

Problem reports

If you send a problem report from the app, we receive the note you write and technical details about the project needed to investigate it.

Purchases

Subscriptions are sold through Stripe. Stripe collects your payment details directly; we never see or store your full card number. We receive your plan, its status and billing history so we can give you the features you paid for.

Usage, diagnostics and device information

  • Product analytics (PostHog): which features are used and counts such as how many words a cut removed, linked to your user ID. We do not send transcript text, video or audio to analytics. Our analytics project is set to discard IP addresses.
  • Crash and performance reports (Sentry): what went wrong, device model, iOS version and app version, linked to your user ID. We configure these reports to leave out transcripts, names and media.
  • Push notification token, if you allow notifications, so we can tell you when a cut or hand-off is ready.
  • An install identifier stored on your iPhone, used to apply usage limits and prevent abuse.

What the app reads on your iPhone

With your permission the app uses the camera and microphone to record, and your photo library to import videos and save finished ones. It only accesses the items you choose or create; it does not upload your photo library.

Face and voice processing (no biometric identifiers)

  • Faces. The retouch and background (green screen) effects use Apple's on-device Vision framework to find where a face or person is in each camera frame, so the effect is drawn in the right place. This happens only on your iPhone, frame by frame, in memory. We do not send it to our servers, store it, build a face template, or use it to recognize or identify anyone.
  • Voices. Our transcription providers label which parts of a single recording were spoken by different people ("speaker 1", "speaker 2") so the app can tell your voice from background voices. We do not create or keep voiceprints, and we do not use voices to identify who someone is.

Because of this, Snip AI does not capture biometric identifiers as defined by Texas law (Tex. Bus. & Com. Code § 503.001) or similar laws. If that ever changes, we will tell you first and ask for your consent.

2. How we use information

  • To provide the service: transcribe audio, decide and apply cuts, store and sync projects, enable team hand-offs, and send notifications.
  • To run your account and subscription, including usage limits and billing.
  • To keep the service working and secure: fix crashes, investigate problem reports, and prevent abuse.
  • To understand which features are used and improve the app. Your transcripts are used to improve cutting only if you turn on "Improve cuts with my fixes".

We do not sell or rent your personal information, we do not use it for advertising, and we do not use your videos, audio or transcripts to train AI models, and we do not allow our providers to.

4. How long we keep information

  • Uploaded audio is deleted as soon as transcription finishes, and in any case within 24 hours.
  • Transcripts and cut records are kept for 30 days, then deleted. If you turn on "Improve cuts with my fixes", the corrections and transcripts you share are kept until you delete your account.
  • Cloud projects are kept while you use them, with these automatic clean-ups: raw takes are removed from the cloud 14 days after a project is approved or exported (unless the team admin chooses to keep them); a deleted project stays in Recently Deleted for 30 days, then is removed; if a paid plan ends, its cloud copies are kept for a limited time with reminders before they are removed. Copies on your iPhone are yours to keep or delete.
  • Account and billing records are kept while your account exists. We may keep limited billing records longer where the law requires it.
  • Diagnostics and analytics are kept for a limited period under our providers' retention settings.

5. Deleting your account and your choices

  • Delete your account in the app under Settings. This deletes your account, your cloud projects (including team projects you own), your transcripts and shared corrections, cancels any subscription, and revokes Sign in with Apple. Copies may remain in encrypted backups for a short time until they are overwritten.
  • Turn off "Improve cuts with my fixes", notifications, or camera, microphone and photo access at any time in Settings.
  • Ask us for a copy of your information, to correct it, or to delete it by emailing turnerstudies@gmail.com. We will answer within 45 days.

Texas and other US state privacy rights

If you live in Texas (under the Texas Data Privacy and Security Act), California, or another state with a consumer privacy law, you have the right to:

  • confirm whether we process your personal data, and get a copy of it in a portable format;
  • correct inaccurate personal data;
  • delete personal data you gave us or that we hold about you; and
  • opt out of the sale of personal data, targeted advertising, and profiling that produces legal or similarly significant effects. We do not do any of these.

We do not sell personal data, including sensitive personal data. The categories of personal data we process, why, and the categories of third parties we share it with are in sections 1 to 3 above.

To make a request, email turnerstudies@gmail.com from the address on your account (or tell us that address). We may need to confirm it is you. You may make a request free of charge at least twice a year, and we will respond within 45 days (we may extend this once by another 45 days if needed, and we will tell you why). We will not treat you differently for using these rights.

To appeal a decision we made about your request, reply to our decision with "Appeal" in the subject within a reasonable time. We will answer your appeal in writing within 60 days and explain our reasons. If you are not satisfied, Texas residents may contact the Texas Attorney General at texasattorneygeneral.gov.

6. Security

Data is encrypted in transit (TLS) and at rest with our storage providers. Our server keys stay on the server, and each account can reach only its own data and the projects its team shares with it. No system is perfectly secure, but we work to protect your information. If a breach affects your personal information, we will notify you as the law requires (in Texas, within 60 days of determining the breach occurred).

7. Children

Snip AI is not directed to children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect information from them. If you believe a child under 13 has given us information, contact us and we will delete it.

8. Where data is processed

Snip AI is offered in the United States. Our providers may process information in the United States and other countries where they operate.

9. Changes to this policy

If we change this policy, we will update the effective date above, and for significant changes we will tell you in the app before they take effect.
